# Privacy Policy

Version: V1.0  
Effective Date: 2026-05-28  
App: Therapist Assistant

Welcome to Therapist Assistant. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data may be processed when you use the app.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to your use of Therapist Assistant and related features, including work logs, notes, special-date reminders, voice broadcast, backup, and restore.

## 2. Data We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

- Data you enter: work entries, notes, special dates, settings, display name, voice/reminder preferences;
- Locally generated data: input history, reminder scheduling state, legal-consent status and timestamps;
- Device/runtime technical data: minimal technical information needed for notifications, backup, ads, and diagnostics (subject to OS and SDK behavior).

## 3. How Data Is Processed

1. The app primarily uses **local storage** for your business data.  
2. Export/Import reads from or writes to local files based on your action.  
3. If you enable notifications, voice, or ads, related permissions and third-party SDKs may process necessary technical data to provide those features.

## 4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data.  
To provide certain features, the app may use third-party services/SDKs (for example, ads, notifications, voice capabilities). Those parties may process data under their own privacy terms.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- Local data retention is primarily controlled by you;  
- You can delete data through in-app deletion, clearing app data, uninstalling, or restore overwrite;  
- Uninstall or device damage may cause irreversible data loss without valid backups.

## 6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection.  
If you are covered by U.S. state privacy laws (including Colorado), you may contact us via "Settings - Technical Support" in the app to submit requests.

## 7. Children

The app is intended for adult professional users (e.g., massage therapists working in the U.S.) and is not directed to children.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and backup files from unauthorized access.

## 9. Policy Updates

We may update this Policy for product, legal, or security reasons. Updates may be posted in-app or via other reasonable notice. Continued use means acceptance of the updated Policy.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us via "Settings - Technical Support" in the app.

## 11. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by applicable U.S. federal law and the laws of the State of Colorado (excluding conflict-of-law principles).

# 隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

版本：V1.0  
生效日期：2026-05-28  
适用应用：按摩师助理（Therapist Assistant）

欢迎使用按摩师助理。我们重视并尊重您的隐私。本隐私政策用于说明我们如何处理与本应用相关的数据。

## 1. 适用范围

本政策适用于您使用按摩师助理移动应用及其相关功能（记工、记事、专属日期提醒、语音播报、备份与导入等）时的数据处理行为。

## 2. 我们处理的数据类型

根据您使用功能的不同，本应用可能处理以下数据：

- 您主动输入的数据：记工记录、记事内容、专属日期、设置项、称呼、语音与提醒配置等；
- 本地生成的数据：输入历史、提醒调度状态、协议同意状态与时间；
- 设备与运行相关信息：为通知、备份、广告展示、崩溃诊断等功能所需的最小必要技术信息（具体以系统与第三方 SDK 实际提供为准）。

## 3. 数据处理方式

1. 本应用以**本地存储**为主，您的业务数据通常保存在设备本地。  
2. 当您使用“导出/导入”功能时，数据会按您的操作写入或读取本地文件。  
3. 当您启用通知、语音或广告功能时，相关系统权限与第三方 SDK 可能处理必要技术数据以完成对应功能。

## 4. 数据共享与第三方

我们不会主动出售您的个人数据。  
为实现特定功能，本应用可能使用第三方服务或 SDK（例如广告、通知、语音能力）。这些第三方可能根据其自身隐私政策处理必要数据。请您同时查阅相关第三方政策。

## 5. 数据保留与删除

- 本地数据保留期限由您控制；  
- 您可通过应用内删除功能、清理应用数据、卸载应用或覆盖导入等方式删除数据；  
- 卸载或设备损坏可能导致数据无法恢复，请您定期备份。

## 6. 您的权利

在适用法律规定范围内，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、数据可携带及反对处理等权利。  
若您位于美国特定州（包括科罗拉多州），您可通过应用“设置 - 技术支持”联系我们行使相关权利。

## 7. 未成年人

本应用面向在美国工作的成年按摩师等职业用户，不以儿童为目标用户。若您认为存在未成年人不当使用情况，请联系我们处理。

## 8. 安全措施

我们采取合理措施降低数据风险，但任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。请您妥善保管设备与备份文件，避免未经授权访问。

## 9. 政策更新

我们可能因产品功能迭代、法律要求或安全需要更新本政策。更新后将通过应用内或其他合理方式提示。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

## 10. 联系我们

如您对本隐私政策或数据处理有疑问，请通过应用“设置 - 技术支持”联系我们。

## 11. 法律适用

本政策受适用的美国联邦法律及科罗拉多州法律管辖（不含冲突规范）。
