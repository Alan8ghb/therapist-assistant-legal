App: Therapist Assistant
Welcome to Therapist Assistant. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data may be processed when you use the app.
1. Scope
This Policy applies to your use of Therapist Assistant and related features, including work logs, notes, special-date reminders, voice broadcast, backup, and restore.
2. Data We May Process
Depending on the features you use, the app may process:
Data you enter: work entries, notes, special dates, settings, display name, voice/reminder preferences;
Locally generated data: input history, reminder scheduling state, legal-consent status and timestamps;
Device/runtime technical data: minimal technical information needed for notifications, backup, ads, and diagnostics.
3. How Data Is Processed
The app primarily uses local storage for your business data.
Export/Import reads from or writes to local files based on your action.
If you enable notifications, voice, or ads, related permissions and third-party SDKs may process necessary technical data to provide those features.
4. Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell your personal data. The app may use third-party services/SDKs (for example, ads, notifications, voice capabilities) to provide specific features.
5. Retention and Deletion
Local data retention is primarily controlled by you;
You can delete data through in-app deletion, clearing app data, uninstalling, or restore overwrite;
Uninstall or device damage may cause irreversible data loss without valid backups.
6. Your Rights
Subject to applicable law, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. You may contact us via "Settings - Technical Support" in the app.
